## Concepts relied upon (identifiers, not copied text)

- Branching-time histories; an agent's choice as a partition cell of
  histories through a moment; `stit` (deliberative: the agent's choice
  guarantees the outcome AND a rejected alternative allowed its absence).
- Agency requires a real choice between alternatives available to THAT agent
  at THAT moment; absence of alternatives never grounds responsibility.
- Model-relativity: STIT verdicts hold in a declared model with declared
  choice partitions, never about the world simpliciter.
