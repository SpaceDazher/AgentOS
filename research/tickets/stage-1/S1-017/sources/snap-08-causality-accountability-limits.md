## Principles relied upon (citations, not copied text)

- Actual causation is model-relative: the same event pattern yields different
  causes under different structural models; a cause verdict never outruns its
  declared model, contingencies and normality assumptions.
- Correlation, temporal ordering and co-occurrence are not causation without
  an explicit model rule (but-for/minimality/contingency discipline).
- Responsibility/blame needs strictly more than causation (intent, knowledge,
  obligation, reasonable alternatives); S1-017 never computes blame —
  legal/moral blame is OUT_OF_SCOPE by contract.
- Provenance records (PROV-DM) are audit substrate: they support
  reconstruction of who/what/when linkages and decide no ALLOW/DENY.
