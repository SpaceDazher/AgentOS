# S1-013 closure limitations

1. No human data: every rate, disposition and N/hour figure is a
   dry-run tooling check on synthetic sessions.
2. SRC-04 was unavailable to the initial preparation agent. R1 obtained the
   local internal source and recorded a portable LF-normalized snapshot and
   both source/snapshot hashes. This is design evidence, not a human study.
3. Planning targets (C1 ≥90% … C5 ≤30s) remain untested hypotheses. The
   planned N=15–20 pilot was cancelled by the operator, not completed.
4. Short approval blocks rescaled per hour prove no stamina;
   subjective fatigue and behavioural errors analyzed separately.
5. Role strata of 8 are not representative; order/learning effects,
   missingness and small-sample power reported, not hidden.
6. Pseudonymisation is not anonymity for small roles and free text;
   manual review required before any release.
7. Reputation, ratings and foreign content never authorize; the
   firewall is structural in tools and protocol.
8. Beta-free design: no numeric reputation model in this ticket.
9. Canonical publication proves record/evidence consistency only; it does not
   convert `human_n=0` into human evidence.
10. The current UI is an accelerated synthetic tooling mode, not the proposed
    75-minute human protocol. Free text stays ungraded without independent
    coding. Synthetic exact-answer controls do not validate human explanations.
11. Baseline full-suite execution exposed a research-evaluation concurrency
    failure outside S1-013; its isolated rerun passed. See REVIEW_R1.md and the
    final corrective verification report for actual subsequent suite outcomes.
12. One operator exercised both owner and reviewer paths in accelerated mode.
    This is a conformance walkthrough, not independent grading or a population
    comprehension/fatigue measurement.
