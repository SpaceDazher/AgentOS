# Facilitator guide (scripted, v1)

## Before the session

- Confirm consent-v1 signed, pseudonymous id assigned, role and block
  order (AB/BA by enrollment parity, seed 13013) recorded.
- Open the mock prototype (local only). Verify the MOCK banner is
  visible and no production system is connected.

## Script (read verbatim, do not paraphrase into hints)

1. "This is a mock system for research. Nothing here is real: no
   real agents, files, or permissions change."
2. "Some tasks are timed. You may stop, skip, or take the offered
   rest at any point with no penalty."
3. Present each comprehension scenario once. Record the primary
   response BEFORE any discussion.
4. For the stop task: start timing at presentation, stop at the
   confirmed stop screen. Show pending/failed acknowledgement as-is;
   never click for the participant.

## Timing moments (log exactly)

task_presented → primary_response_recorded → stop_requested →
stop_confirmed. Use the prototype's event log; do not estimate.

## Do / do not

- Do record opt-outs, confusion, fatigue remarks and deviations.
- Do not explain correct answers until the debrief block.
- Do not exclude poor understanding; record it and continue or stop
  per participant wish.
- On discomfort, technical failure or withdrawal: stop, note the
  reason, preserve partial data per the missing/censoring policy.
